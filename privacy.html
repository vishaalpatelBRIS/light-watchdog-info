Light Watchdog

Privacy policy

Light Watchdog is a personal-use Windows application operated locally by its owner. This policy describes the application's optional Google connection and light-failure emails.

Google account data

With the owner's consent, the application receives a verified Google account email address and authorization tokens. It uses the email address to ensure the selected account matches the configured sender. It uses the tokens only to send test and light-failure emails through Gmail and renew the authorized connection.

The requested permissions are openid, userinfo.email, and gmail.send. The application does not request inbox-reading access and does not retrieve existing emails, contacts, calendar entries, or Drive files.

Alert contents and sharing

Failure emails may contain configured light names, hardware addresses, scheduled actions and times, attempt counts, and failure status. They are transmitted to Google for email delivery and to the recipient selected by the owner. The application does not sell Google account data, use it for advertising, or send it to an application-operated cloud server.

Light Watchdog's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and retention

Google authorization tokens and app credentials are stored locally, encrypted using Windows Data Protection for the signed-in Windows user. Local configuration, light state, delivery records, and diagnostic logs are also kept on that computer. The application does not intentionally put passwords or tokens in its logs or source repository.

The owner controls these local files and may disable alerts and remove the stored connection. Stored credentials remain until removed or replaced. Sent email is retained by Google and the recipient according to their settings; deleting local files does not delete delivered emails.

Disconnecting and deletion

The owner can revoke access using Google Account connections. To remove local authorization data, disable email alerts and delete google-token.bin and google-client.bin from the owner's TapoWatchdog folder. Local configuration, state, and logs can be removed separately after stopping the application.

This website

These information pages contain no application sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy policy.
